# AI Layer

Honba includes an AI feedback layer that no other Indian-market
open-source trading platform currently offers. It is also the layer
most likely to be misunderstood.

## The Documents

- **[Research Loop](research_loop.md)** — an LLM reads a journal of
  past hypotheses and their outcomes, and generates new hypotheses
  informed by what has already been tried.
- **[RL Training](rl_training.md)** — training reinforcement learning
  agents on the backtest simulator.
- **[MCP Gateway](mcp_gateway.md)** — exposing Honba's capabilities to
  external AI agents via Model Context Protocol.
- **[NL Verification](nl_verification.md)** — describing a strategy in
  plain English, having Honba implement it, and getting a
  plain-English critique.

## What the AI Layer Is For

The AI layer accelerates research. It does not replace it.

- The **research loop** generates hypotheses faster than a human can.
  Most are wrong. The ones that survive the same validation suite a
  human-written strategy would face are worth looking at.
- **RL training** is useful for narrow problems (position sizing
  given a signal, execution optimization). It is not a reliable path
  to signal discovery.
- The **MCP gateway** lets an AI agent drive Honba. It is the
  mechanism that turns Honba from a tool you use into a tool your
  agent uses.
- **NL verification** is a checklist generator. It surfaces concerns
  you might not have thought of. It does not judge whether a
  strategy is good.

## What the AI Layer Is Not

- It is not a strategy generator on demand.
- It is not a replacement for the validation suite.
- It does not have access to broker credentials or live trading.

The AI layer's scope is research. Acting on research remains a human
decision.

## Honest Expectations

Of 100 hypotheses generated by the research loop:

- ~60 fail Tier 1 (obvious non-starters).
- ~35 fail Tier 2 (unrealistic after costs).
- ~4 fail the validation suite.
- **~1 survives.**

That one is worth paper trading. It is not worth deploying capital to
without further review. This ratio is not a limitation of the AI
layer; it is what quantitative research looks like.

If the AI layer produces 10 "winners" out of 100 hypotheses, the
validation suite is not being applied correctly.
